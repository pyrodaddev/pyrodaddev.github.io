How to play

Every board is split into coloured lairs. Place one dragon in every row, every column and every lair. Dragons are territorial: no two may touch, not even diagonally.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by logic alone. No guessing needed.

Controls

  • Tap a square to scorch it (✕), tap again for a dragon, again to clear.
  • Long-press to place or remove a dragon straight away.
  • Drag across squares to scorch lots at once.

Dragon fire

With dragon fire on, a placed dragon scorches every square it rules out, so you can see the board narrow down. Turn it off in Settings (or on the toolbar) for a purer challenge.

The Sage

Stuck? The Sage explains the next logical step in plain words and can show you where it applies. You get 3 free hints a day.

The daily puzzle

There is one new daily puzzle for everyone, every day, and it resets at midnight on your phone. The board grows through the week: 7×7 on Monday and Tuesday, 8×8 on Wednesday and Thursday, 9×9 on Friday and Saturday, and a 10×10 on Sunday. The board stays hidden until you tap Start, so take your time getting ready. When you finish, you see how your time compares with everyone else who solved it today.

Practice

Five practice tiers, from Ember (6×6) to Inferno (10×10), with hundreds of puzzles each. Practice puzzles earn embers; every 10 embers warm a new egg.

Hatching dragons

Solving the daily puzzle earns an egg. Solve it flawlessly (without breaking a rule or using a hint) and it’s a lucky egg, with better odds of something rare. There are 30 species: five lineages in six elements (Ember, Tide, Moss, Stone, Storm and Frost), plus limited-time event dragons. Any dragon can also hatch Gilded, a rare golden version.

You can’t buy eggs or dragons. Every one is earned by solving puzzles. These are the exact odds:

Rare and Golden eggs

Every puzzle you solve, daily or practice, also has a small chance of dropping a bonus egg. You can find at most one bonus egg a day, and each puzzle’s chance is fixed, so replaying a puzzle can’t roll it again.

Bonus eggChance per solveWhat hatches
Golden egg1 in 200 Always Gilded. Its rarity uses the daily egg odds above.
Rare egg1 in 50 Rare 75%, Epic 20%, Legendary 5%, with a 5% chance of Gilded.

Rare and Golden eggs can hatch in any of the six elements.

Serial numbers

When you’re online, every new dragon is registered and gets a worldwide serial number for its species, so you know whether you own #7 of 12 or #30,551 of 34,000. The Dragodex shows how many of each species exist across all players.

Companions and Ascension

Choose any dragon as your Companion. Every daily puzzle you solve in a row together builds your bond, and long bonds make your Companion ascend to a stronger form:

Hatchlingwhen it hatches
Fledgling10 days in a row
Adult100 days
Elder365 days
Ancient1,000 days

A dragon keeps the stages it has reached. Switching Companions ends the current bond and starts a fresh one with the new dragon. The game asks first if you’d lose a bond.

Can’t wait for Fledgling? Open any Hatchling in your Lair and you can choose to watch three short ads to make it a Fledgling straight away (your progress is saved between ads). Its details note that it grew up early. This only works for that first step: Adult, Elder and Ancient can only be reached with a long bond.

Streaks, shields and rekindling

  • Every 7-day streak earns an Ember Shield (you can hold 3). A shield covers a missed day automatically.
  • Missed a day with no shield? Your streak goes cold, but on the day you come back you can rekindle it by watching one optional ad, before or after that day’s puzzle. Your Companion’s bond comes back with it.

Leaderboards

Three leaderboards are updated every few minutes:

  • Today’s fastest: your daily puzzle time from tapping Start to placing your last dragon, measured by our server’s clock. It doesn’t pause, so only tap Start when you’re ready. Solves that used the Sage’s hints aren’t timed on this board.
  • Streaks: days in a row with a timed daily solve. Ember Shields protect your own streak, but not this one.
  • Collectors: how many different species you’ve registered online, with event dragons as a bonus.

To appear on them, choose a Tamer name on the leaderboard screen or in Settings. It’s optional. Your name gets a four-digit number, like Ember#4821, so lots of players can share a name. You can change it once every 20 hours. Leaderboard times need a connection when you tap Start and when you finish.

Keep names friendly: no rude or hateful words, and no real names or contact details. If you see a name that breaks these rules, tap it on the leaderboard to report it. Names that break the rules are hidden, and their owner can then choose a new one. To remove your Tamer name altogether, email us.

Events

Special events bring dragons you can only hatch for a short time. Hallow’s Eve runs from 24 October to 1 November 2026: every solve, daily or practice, has a 12% chance to drop a Hallowed egg, and solving the daily on 31 October guarantees one. A Hallowed egg hatches Gourdgrin (Rare, 80%) or Hollowing (Epic, 20%), with a 3% chance of Gilded. Event dragons are a bonus and don’t count towards the 30 in the Dragodex.

Ads, fairly

  • No ads during a puzzle, ever, and none at all for your first five solves.
  • After that, at most one short ad after every three practice puzzles, at least four minutes apart, and no more than six a day. Never after the daily puzzle.
  • Optional ads (three extra hints, rekindling a streak, or growing a Hatchling into a Fledgling) only play if you choose them.
  • A one-off Remove Ads purchase switches the between-puzzle ads off for good and makes Sage hints unlimited.

Questions

How do I redeem a gift code?

Open Settings → Redeem a gift code and type the code. Codes from PyroDadDev contain a specific dragon, sometimes a special one, or an egg to hatch. Each code works once, and you need to be online to redeem it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything you play is saved on your phone. Serial numbers and worldwide counts catch up the next time you’re online, and your daily time joins the rankings if you’re back online by the next day. Leaderboard times are the exception: they need a connection when you tap Start and when you finish.

I’ve changed phones. Can I bring my dragons?

There are no accounts, so your Lair lives on your phone. If your phone’s Google backup is switched on, Android usually includes Dragodoku’s saved game, so it comes back when you set up a new Android phone from that backup (it may be up to a day old). There’s no other way to move it yet, sorry. If you bought Remove Ads, use Settings → Restore purchase on your new phone to get it back.

Is the daily puzzle the same for everyone?

Yes. Everyone gets the same board on the same date, which is what makes the rankings fair.

Something’s wrong, or I have an idea

Email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com. If it’s about a dragon, include its name and serial number from the card.
