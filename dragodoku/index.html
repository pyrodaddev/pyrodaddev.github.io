How to play

Every board is split into coloured lairs. Place one dragon in every row, every column and every lair. Dragons are territorial: no two may touch, not even diagonally.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by logic alone. No guessing needed.

New to puzzles like this? The app’s guided puzzle walks you through a small board move by move, in about a minute. It’s offered when you first open the app, and you can play it any time from How to play or Settings.

Controls

  • Tap a square to scorch it (✕), tap again for a dragon, again to clear.
  • Long-press to place or remove a dragon straight away.
  • Drag across squares to scorch lots at once.

Dragon fire

With dragon fire on, a placed dragon scorches every square it rules out, so you can see the board narrow down. Turn it off in Settings (or on the toolbar) for a purer challenge.

The Sage

Stuck? The Sage explains the next logical step in plain words and can show you where it applies. You get 3 free hints a day, shared by every board. Whenever a hint is open, a short optional ad adds 3 more.

Today’s Five

Every day brings five new puzzles, the same for everyone: Glow (6×6), Spark (7×7), Flame (8×8), Blaze (9×9) and the toughest, Inferno (10×10). They reset at midnight on your phone. Solve any one of them to keep your streak going and to hatch the day’s egg; every puzzle you solve also earns embers. Solve all five on the same day for a second egg, the Perfect Day egg. Each board stays hidden until you tap Start, so take your time getting ready. When you finish, you see how your time compares with everyone else who solved that puzzle today.

Past puzzles

Missed a day? The last 7 days’ puzzles stay open under Past puzzles on the home screen. They earn embers and the usual bonus-egg chance, but no leaderboard time, streak, day’s egg or Perfect Day egg.

Handy extras

  • See a solved board: tap any puzzle you’ve solved (today’s or in Past puzzles) to see the finished board, ready to screenshot. The Share button sends your result without the solution.
  • Home-screen widget (Android): long-press your home screen, choose Widgets and add Dragodoku to see your streak and today’s five at a glance. It turns over to the new day at midnight.
  • Back up with Google: keep your dragons safe in case you lose or change your phone (see Questions below).
  • Colour-blind friendly lairs: in Settings, switch on clearer colours with a pattern in each lair. The Sage then names lairs by their pattern (“the Dotted lair”).
  • Dragodex and cards: tap a dragon you’ve found in the Dragodex to open its card, and the book button on any card takes you to its Dragodex entry. Species you haven’t found stay hidden.
  • Shortcuts: long-press the app icon for “Bugs? Contact us”.

Practice and embers

The same five sizes, with hundreds of practice puzzles each. Every solve (daily, past or practice) earns embers, 1 for Glow up to 5 for Inferno, and every 10 embers lay an ember egg. Your embers show at the top of the home screen: tap them to see how close the next egg is and where to earn more. Embers can’t be bought.

Hatching dragons

Your first daily puzzle of the day earns an egg. Solve it flawlessly (without breaking a rule or using a hint) and it’s a lucky egg, with better odds of something rare. Solve all five of the day’s puzzles and you also earn a Perfect Day egg, with better odds still. There are 30 species: five lineages in six elements (Fire, Tide, Moss, Stone, Storm and Frost), plus limited-time event dragons. Any dragon can also hatch Gilded, a rare golden version.

You can’t buy eggs or dragons. Every one is earned by solving puzzles. These are the exact odds:

Rare and Golden eggs

Every puzzle you solve (daily, past or practice) also has a small chance of dropping a bonus egg. You can find at most one bonus egg a day, and each puzzle’s chance is fixed, so replaying a puzzle can’t roll it again.

Bonus eggChance per solveWhat hatches
Golden egg1 in 200 Always Gilded. Its rarity uses the daily egg odds above.
Rare egg1 in 50 Rare 75%, Epic 20%, Legendary 5%, with a 5% chance of Gilded.

Rare and Golden eggs can hatch in any of the six elements.

Serial numbers

When you’re online, every new dragon is registered and gets a worldwide serial number for its species. Serials come in runs of 9,999: the very first of a species is #AAA-0001, the 9,999th is #AAA-9999, then the next run starts at #AAB-0001, all the way to ZZZ. A dragon from the first run, AAA, is one of the earliest of its kind. The Dragodex shows how many of each species exist across all players.

Companions and Ascension

Choose any dragon as your Companion. Every day in a row you solve at least one daily puzzle together builds your bond, and long bonds make your Companion ascend to a stronger form:

When your Companion is ready, you get to watch it ascend: tap Ascend and it changes into its new form. You can share the moment as a picture. A dragon keeps the stages it has reached. Switching Companions ends the current bond and starts a fresh one with the new dragon. The game asks first if you’d lose a bond.

Can’t wait for Fledgling? Open any Hatchling in your Lair and you can choose to watch three short ads to make it a Fledgling straight away (your progress is saved between ads). Its details note that it grew up early. This only works for that first step: Adult, Elder, Ancient and Eternal can only be reached with a long bond.

Streaks, shields and rekindling

  • Every 7-day streak earns a Streak Shield (you can hold 3). A shield covers a missed day automatically.
  • Missed a day with no shield? Your streak goes cold, but on the day you come back you can rekindle it by watching one optional ad, before or after that day’s puzzle. Your Companion’s bond comes back with it.

Dragon Duels

Battle your dragons card against card. Open Dragon Duels on the home screen once you have five dragons.

  • Your deck is up to 12 of your dragons (your strongest, unless you pick them yourself). Each duel deals you 7 of them at random.
  • Five rounds. Each round, the caller names a stat (Might, Guard, Swift or Spirit), then you both play a dragon in secret. The higher stat wins the round. Win the most rounds to win the duel: three wins settle it early, and a tied round scores nothing.
  • Each stat can be called twice at most, and the loser of a round calls the next one.
  • Elements: a dragon whose element beats its rival's gets +20% that round. Fire beats Frost, Frost beats Moss, Moss beats Stone, Stone beats Storm, Storm beats Tide, and Tide beats Fire.
  • Ascension counts, gently: +10% a stage, up to +50% for an Eternal, and +5% for a Gilded dragon, so young dragons still stand a chance.
  • Level after five rounds? Sudden death with your spare dragons. Still level: a draw.

Three rivals: Dai the Apprentice, Seren the Tamer (a fair match for your deck) and Gwyn the Dragonlord (stronger, and sharp). Each win earns embers and Scales for your first five wins a day, and every ten prize wins lay a Lucky egg. Scales can't be bought: keep them for the dragon cosmetics coming soon.

Other Tamers: with a Tamer name you can duel the decks of other Tamers near your strength (the computer plays their dragons, with Gwyn’s sharp mind; a win is worth +3 embers and +3 Scales), and share your own deck for them to take on. Sharing shows your Tamer name and your deck’s dragons (never the names you gave them); switch it off any time in Dragon Duels.

Duel a friend, live (from version 1.9.0): in Dragon Duels, one of you taps Open a room and shares the six-character code; the other taps Join a friend and enters it within 30 minutes. You each play your own deck from your own phone. Your dragon stays hidden until you’ve both played, so neither of you can peek. A win counts toward the day’s five prize wins (+3 embers and +3 Scales); leaving part-way counts as nothing for either of you. You both need a Tamer name, so you know who you’re playing.

Leaderboards

The leaderboards are updated every few minutes:

  • Today’s fastest, one board for each of the five puzzles: your time from tapping Start to placing your last dragon, measured by our server’s clock. It doesn’t pause, so only tap Start when you’re ready. Solves that used the Sage’s hints aren’t timed on these boards.
  • Streaks: days in a row with a timed daily solve. Streak Shields protect your own streak, but not this one.
  • Collectors: how many different species you’ve registered online, with event dragons as a bonus.

You can share your day as a picture: your five puzzles, your Companion and its progress to the next stage, and your streak. Ascensions can be shared the same way. To appear on the leaderboards, choose a Tamer name on the leaderboard screen or in Settings. It’s optional. Your name gets a four-digit number, like Nimbus#4821, so lots of players can share a name. You can change it once every 20 hours. Leaderboard times need a connection when you tap Start and when you finish.

Keep names friendly: no rude or hateful words, and no real names or contact details. If you see a name that breaks these rules, tap it on the leaderboard to report it. Names that break the rules are hidden, and their owner can then choose a new one. To remove your Tamer name altogether, email us.

Events

Special events bring dragons you can only hatch for a short time. Hallow’s Eve runs from 24 October to 1 November 2026: every solve, daily or practice, has a 12% chance to drop a Hallowed egg, and solving the daily on 31 October guarantees one. A Hallowed egg hatches Gourdgrin (Rare, 80%) or Hollowing (Epic, 20%), with a 3% chance of Gilded. Event dragons are a bonus and don’t count towards the 30 in the Dragodex.

Ads, fairly

  • No ads during a puzzle, ever, and none at all for your first five solves.
  • After that, at most one short ad after every three practice or past puzzles, at least four minutes apart, and no more than six a day. Never after one of today’s five.
  • Optional ads (three extra hints, rekindling a streak, or growing a Hatchling into a Fledgling) only play if you choose them.
  • A one-off Remove Ads purchase switches the between-puzzle ads off for good and makes Sage hints unlimited.

Questions

How do I redeem a gift code?

Open Settings → Redeem a gift code and type the code. Codes from PyroDadDev contain a specific dragon, sometimes a special one, or an egg to hatch. Each code works once, and you need to be online to redeem it.

What’s an air drop?

Every so often PyroDadDev drops a surprise egg or dragon into the games of players picked at random from those who solved a daily puzzle recently, and sometimes sends a gift to one player. There’s nothing to type: it lands in your nest the next time you open the game while online, with a note from us. Air drops are free, can’t be bought, and the more often you play, the more likely you are to be in the draw.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything you play is saved on your phone. Serial numbers and worldwide counts catch up the next time you’re online, and your daily times join the rankings if you’re back online by the next day. Leaderboard times are the exception: they need a connection when you tap Start and when you finish.

I’ve changed phones. Can I bring my dragons?

Yes, if you back up first. Open Settings → Back up with Google (or tap the card on the home screen) and pick your Google account. From then on the game saves your whole Lair to it by itself: every dragon and its stage, your streak, eggs, embers and Tamer name. It’s optional, and the backup is private to your Google sign-in.

On your new phone, install Dragodoku and tap Played before? Restore your dragons on the first screen (or Settings → Back up with Google later) with the same Google account, then Restore. You come back as the same player, with the same serial numbers and leaderboard name. If the new phone already has dragons, you choose which Lair to keep. If you bought Remove Ads, use Settings → Restore purchase to get it back too.

Stop backing up signs out and keeps the backup ready to restore. Delete my backup removes it, and your sign-in, from our server. See the privacy policy for exactly what is kept.

Are the daily puzzles the same for everyone?

Yes. Everyone gets the same five boards on the same date, which is what makes the rankings fair.

Something’s wrong, or I have an idea

Email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com. If it’s about a dragon, include its name and serial number from the card.
