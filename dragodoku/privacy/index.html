Privacy Policy

Dragodoku · by PyroDadDev · Effective 11 October 2026

In short: Dragodoku has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress and dragons are saved on your phone. To show daily rankings and give each dragon a worldwide serial number, the game uses an anonymous player ID and sends a few game facts to our Google Firebase database. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.

Who we are

Dragodoku (“the game”) is made by PyroDadDev (“we”, “us”), published on Google Play as Dragon Developments, an independent developer based in Wales, United Kingdom. We are the controller of the game data described below. For anything about this policy or your data, email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com.

Information kept on your device

The game stores the following only on your device. It is not sent to us:

  • your puzzle progress: daily results and times, streaks, Ember Shields, unfinished boards, practice progress and embers;
  • your eggs and dragons, including any names you give them, and which one is your Companion;
  • your settings: dragon fire, timer, sound, haptics and the reminder time;
  • a random install code used to roll your eggs, gift codes you’ve redeemed, how many hints you’ve used today, and counters that keep ads to the limits we promise;
  • whether you have bought “Remove Ads”.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Rankings and the dragon registry (Google Firebase)

When your phone is online, the game signs in to our Firebase project with Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This creates a random player ID for your copy of the game. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or Google account, and we don’t try to identify you. The game then sends only this:

WhenWhat is storedWhy
You solve the daily puzzle The puzzle date and your solve time, kept with your anonymous player ID, plus an anonymous count of solve times and flawless solves for that day. To show “faster than X% of players”, count each player once, and tell you your finishing order.
A dragon hatches, or later ascends Its species, stage, whether it is Gilded, its serial number, what kind of egg it came from, the date it hatched and your anonymous player ID. Running totals per species are updated. To give every dragon a unique worldwide serial number and show how many of each species exist.
You redeem a gift code That the code has been used, and by which anonymous player ID, plus the dragon’s registry record. So each code works once.

The per-species totals and the daily counts are public inside the game and contain no IDs. Firebase is provided by Google, which processes this data for us as our service provider; it also handles your IP address and basic device information to deliver the service and keep it secure. The database is hosted in Google Cloud’s London region (europe-west2). See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

We keep these records while the game is available, because serial numbers must stay unique. You can ask us to delete yours at any time (see below).

Deleting your data

Data on your phone: uninstall Dragodoku. This deletes your progress, dragons, settings and the anonymous sign-in on that device.

Data in our Firebase database: email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com with the subject “Dragodoku data deletion” and include the name and serial number shown on at least one of your dragon cards (for example “Cinderpip #0042”). We use it to find your anonymous player ID, then delete every dragon registry record and daily-ranking entry linked to that ID within 30 days, and confirm by email.

What is kept: the per-species totals and daily solver counts, which contain no IDs, and the serial numbers themselves, which are never reissued. Ad data is held by Google; use the ad choices below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, and optional ads you can choose to watch for extra hints or to rekindle a streak. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects. To serve ads, limit repeats, measure performance and prevent fraud, the SDK may collect:

DataExamples
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID; on iPhone, the ID for vendors (Dragodoku does not ask for iPhone tracking permission)
Approximate locationderived from your IP address
Ad interactionswhich ads were shown, watched or tapped
Diagnosticsdevice type, operating system, crash and performance data

Learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: on first launch you see Google’s consent message, with the choice to consent, not consent, or manage options. Personalised ads are only shown if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads on some devices).
  • Fewer ads: the one-off “Remove Ads” purchase switches off the between-puzzle ads for good. Optional ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

“Remove Ads” is sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle payment. We never receive your payment details, only confirmation that the purchase was made, so the game can switch ads off and restore the purchase on a new device.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules it locally and shows it at the time you choose. No notification data is sent to us, and we don’t use push notifications. The game asks for notification permission only when you turn the reminder on.

Sharing

If you tap Share, your device’s share sheet opens with a short message (your result or your dragon) and sometimes a picture of your dragon card. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Legal bases (UK GDPR and EU GDPR)

Rankings, the dragon registry and gift codes are part of the game you asked to play, and rely on our legitimate interests in running fair rankings and unique serial numbers with as little data as possible. Where consent is required, personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Non-personalised ads, frequency limits, fraud prevention and measurement rely on legitimate interests: keeping the game free and secure.

Children

Dragodoku is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because the game is anonymous, we can only find your records from the dragon names and serial numbers you send us. Ad data is handled by Google via the links above. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game’s store listing.
