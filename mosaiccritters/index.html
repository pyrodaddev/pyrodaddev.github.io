Mosaic Critters

A daily logic puzzle. Every solve reveals a pixel critter.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

Every number counts the filled cells in the little square around it. Work out which cells are filled by logic alone, and a pixel-art critter appears. Three new boards every day, the same for everyone, and every daily critter you reveal joins your album.

What's inside

  • Three daily boards: Small 10×10, Medium 13×13 and Large 16×16
  • Pure logic, no guessing: every board has exactly one answer
  • 96 critters to collect, from common to legendary, with a rare one every Sunday
  • Unlimited practice boards, a streak to keep, and no account needed
Early days: Mosaic Critters is in closed testing on Google Play, so the store page may say it isn't available yet. To join the testers, email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com with the Google account you use on your phone, then open the opt-in link.

Help and contact

Questions, ideas or a bug to report? Email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com. Your progress lives on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it; the privacy policy explains the ads and your choices.
