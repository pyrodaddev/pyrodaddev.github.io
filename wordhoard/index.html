Wordhoard

A themed word search every day. Find the words, stamp your hoard.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

Every day brings a new theme, from Welsh castles to things in a toolbox. Find its words hidden in the grid, in any of eight directions, by dragging from the first letter to the last. Finish the day's board and its stamp goes into your hoard.

What's inside

  • Two boards a day on the same theme: Quick 10×10 and Full 13×13
  • Over 400 themes, with treasured ones on Sundays
  • A stamp for every theme you complete, and a streak to keep
  • Practice boards whenever you like, and no account needed
Early days: Wordhoard is in closed testing on Google Play, so the store page may say it isn't available yet. To join the testers, email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com with the Google account you use on your phone, then open the opt-in link.

Help and contact

Questions, ideas or a bug to report? Email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com. Your progress lives on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it; the privacy policy explains the ads and your choices.
