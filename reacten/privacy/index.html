Privacy Policy

Reacten Arena · by PyroDadDev · Effective 10 October 2026

In short: Reacten Arena has no accounts and sends none of your game data to us. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve and measure those ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.

Who we are

Reacten Arena (“the game”) is made by PyroDadDev (“we”, “us”), published on Google Play as Dragon Developments, an independent developer based in Wales, United Kingdom. For anything about this policy or your data, email pyrodaddev@googlegroups.com.

Information the game keeps on your device

The game stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it:

  • your runs: times, misses, target counts, dates, personal bests and Daily Arena streaks;
  • your settings: sound, haptics, theme, target size, target colour and region;
  • whether you have bought “Remove Ads”, so the game stays ad-free offline.

You can delete your runs at any time with Settings → Reset local stats. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you buy “Remove Ads”, the game shows an occasional full-screen ad between games, provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects. To serve ads, limit repeat ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, the SDK may collect:

DataExamples
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID; on iPhone, the ID for vendors (Reacten Arena does not ask for iPhone tracking permission, so your iPhone advertising identifier is not shared)
Approximate locationderived from your IP address
Ad interactionswhich ads were shown or tapped
Diagnosticsdevice type, operating system, crash and performance data

Learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: on first launch you see Google’s consent message, and personalised ads are only shown if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads on some devices).
  • No ads at all: the one-off “Remove Ads” purchase switches ads off for good. After that purchase the ads SDK is never started.

In-app purchases

“Remove Ads” is sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They handle payment, and we never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase was made so it can switch ads off and restore them on a new device.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share, your device’s share sheet opens with a short text message containing your time. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Legal bases (UK GDPR and EU GDPR)

Where consent is required, personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Non-personalised ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement rely on legitimate interests: keeping the game free and secure. The data kept on your device is used only to provide the game features you use.

Children

Reacten Arena is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests are best handled by Google for ad data, via the links above, or on your own device. You can also complain to your data-protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game’s store listing.
